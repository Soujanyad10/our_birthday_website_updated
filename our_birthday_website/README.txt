OUR BIRTHDAY WEBSITE 💗

PREVIEW: Open happybirthdaymylove.html in Chrome. Keep the assets folder beside it.

ADD GALLERY PHOTOS: Put six photos in assets/photos named 01.jpg through 06.jpg.
OPTIONAL GIFT PHOTOS: Add leather-jacket.jpg (him wearing the jacket), bracelet.jpg, and photo-frame.jpg in assets/photos.
ADD MUSIC: Put your chosen MP3 at assets/our-song.mp3. It starts when he opens the secret envelope; browser autoplay rules may require a click.

PERSONALISE: Open happybirthdaymylove.html in VS Code or a text editor. Near the bottom, edit CONFIG:
- milestones: replace sample dates and descriptions with your real relationship timeline
- photos: edit captions/filenames if needed
- letter: replace the sample letter with your own words
- songFile: change if you use a different MP3 filename

GIFT BUTTONS: The bracelet and photo-frame buttons show a playful birthday confirmation and a two-day surprise message. This is a website animation, not live courier tracking.

PUBLISH: Visit https://app.netlify.com/drop, sign in, then drag the complete website folder (including assets) onto the page. The birthday page URL ends in /happybirthdaymylove.html. Anyone with the URL may be able to open it, so share privately.
